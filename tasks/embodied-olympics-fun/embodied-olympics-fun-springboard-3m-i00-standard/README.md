## Task
Do a forward dive straight (101A) from the 3 m springboard: from your standing start at its end, take off from both feet, turn half a somersault forwards in the straight position and enter the water head first, vertical, with your arms stretched beyond your head. (Embodied Olympics `springboard-3m`; the robot as a service: its head cameras, joint sensors and IMUs, and the venue's fixed camera only.)

## Difficulty
The robot must time its push with a board that bends and recoils under its 33 kg, take off high with exactly the right forward rotation, and then hold a straight body with the legs drawn together, the toes pointed and the short arms stretched overhead through a 1 s fall. Nothing can be corrected once it is in the air. In standard mode the agent has no simulator and no state: it sees through the robot's cameras, acts through the client, and has 5 resets (only the last attempt is graded).

## Reference solution
`solution/oracle_replay.py` streams the privileged twin's reference solution, row by row, through the client (`solution/rows.npz`, recorded at generation time). Reference: a scripted dive (ankle-strategy balance on the bent board while the arms rise, a balanced crouch, a 0.27 s lean, a push that depresses the board 0.18 m), the lean time found by bisection: 170 deg at the first touch, 5.3 deg at the hips, entry 1.44 m out. The board has a tip stiffness of 5449 N/m (built for a published 5446) and a first mode of 3.93 Hz (sqrt(k/m_eff) 3.94). Referee test negatives as in platform-10m plus a double bounce; nop topples off at 18.5 s (a fall). The privileged twin's reference rows (567 control steps, success after 5.67 s) streamed through the client.

## Verification
The sim sidecar's referee judges the live attempt, and that is the verdict (`final.json`: `graded_by` live, `replays` 0;
no grading replay, owner 2026-10-17); the frozen start is checked on the live simulator (`instance_mismatch`:
infrastructure), and the video is rendered from the recorded attempt afterwards. `/tests/grade.py` reads
`/data/final.json`: pass iff the live attempt succeeded. The
rules: You stand at the end of the 3 m springboard facing the water (+x). The board bends and springs back under you: you may rock it with your feet on it. The dive: take off from both feet at once (both feet stay on the board until the take-off: a foot off it for more than 0.1 s while the other is on it, or any flight of 0.1 s or more that lands on the board again (a double bounce), fails; the take-off counts when you are in the air and your centre of mass has risen at least 0.1 m above its height at the start), turn forwards through half a somersault (180.0 degrees, within 45.0, counted from your upright start) and enter the water head first. From the moment your body line (from the middle of your ankles to the middle of your shoulders) passes the horizontal until the dive is complete you must be in the straight position: hips and knees each bent less than 20.0 degrees from straight, the ankles at most 0.12 m apart, the toes pointed (each ankle pitched at least 0.3 rad towards the toes). The entry starts when the first part of you reaches the water surface (z = 0) and the dive is complete when the whole of you is under it; that first part must be a hand or the head (feet first fails); when your pelvis reaches the surface your body line must be within 15.0 degrees of vertical, head down; through the entry: not twisted (your shoulders' axis within 20.0 degrees of the board's width direction); both palms at least 0.15 m beyond the centre of your head along the body line, at most 0.2 m apart and each within 0.15 m of the body line; and the straight position. The entry point (the middle of your palms when the first part of you touches the water) must be between 0.5 m and 3.0 m in front of the board's tip at rest (closer is unsafely close) and within 0.5 m to either side of the line you face at the start. You fail if, after the take-off, any part of you touches the board, its stand or the railings. Before the take-off any part other than the feet on the board, or anything on the railings, fails. The water is not simulated: nothing after the dive is complete is judged. Success: a complete dive that keeps all of these. The attempt ends after 20 s.
